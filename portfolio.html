About the Project

Gramin Setu is a multilingual, voice-assisted web platform developed to make digital government services easier to access for farmers and rural communities.

Users can select a supported language and speak a command. The prototype recognizes the spoken request, identifies the intended service and navigates to its official public portal.

Problem Statement

Many rural citizens face difficulties navigating digital government services because of language barriers, unfamiliar interfaces and limited digital literacy.

Our Solution

Gramin Setu explores a simple voice-first interface that helps users reach relevant government portals using spoken commands.

Key Features

🎙️ Voice Commands

Access services through spoken instructions.

🌐 Language Selection

Language options include English, Hindi, Marathi and Kannada.

🔍 Service Recognition

Recognizes supported voice requests and identifies the intended service.

🏛️ Government Portals

Provides navigation to official portals such as DigiLocker, Aadhaar and PM-KISAN.

📱 Simple Interface

A straightforward interface designed with accessibility in mind.

🌱 Digital Inclusion

Explores ways to make digital services more approachable for rural communities.

How It Works

01. User selects a language.
02. User presses the Speak Now button.
03. Browser speech recognition captures the command.
04. The prototype identifies a supported service.
05. The browser navigates to the corresponding official portal.

Project Demonstration

During testing, the Hindi command "DigiLocker खोलिए" successfully identified the DigiLocker service and opened its official portal.

This is a working prototype demonstrating voice-based service navigation. It does not directly integrate with government databases or bypass official authentication procedures.
